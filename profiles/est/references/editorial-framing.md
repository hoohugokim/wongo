# ES&T editorial framing (seed — expand)

- Desk screen tests novelty (new data / new interpretation / new analysis with
  environmental insight) and breadth of impact; routine data is declined.
- Environmental relevance must be built into methods, data, and uncertainty
  treatment — not asserted in the final paragraph.
- Cover letter is a fit-argument, not a summary: name the environmental system,
  the insight, and why ES&T's readership specifically.
